# Tending Dry Run

## Grounding

```yaml
id: grounding
duration_min: 13
theme_anchor: >-
  We just had a full moon. From here the moon wanes, the evenings get darker, and many of us start
  turning inward. Tonight is about gathering energy to sustain you through the winter. In each
  shape, settle in at a level you could stay in for a while.
yin_principles:
  - Find your first edge by waiting for it rather than reaching for it. Start shallower than you think.
  - Sensation between 2 and 5 out of 10. Dull, warm, spread out means stay. Sharp, electric, pinching or pinpoint means back off.
  - Once you are in, stop managing. Let the props do the holding.
guided_silent_ratio: >-
  Two minutes of propping, naming that anyone can come out to sitting, Child's, or onto their side.
  Seven minutes in Child's with the breath into the back of the ribs, turning the head halfway.
  Four minutes of principles and scale while resting. A late start comes out of the time in Child's.
x_childs_setup: >-
  Bolster lengthwise between the knees, under the belly, chest, and head. Knees as wide as the body
  likes, with room for the belly. Folded blanket under the knees. Turn the head halfway through.
x_childs_settling: >-
  Scan the knees first. If you feel pressure or pinching, add blanket between hips and heels or take
  the knees wider. Side-lying with the bolster between the knees is always available.
```

## Transition: To Supported Caterpillar

```yaml
id: transition-to-supported-caterpillar
duration_min: 4
next_segment_id: supported-caterpillar
setup: >-
  Slide the bolster aside and come to all fours. Round and lift the spine with the breath, rock the
  hips back toward the heels and forward, then walk the hands to each side. Then sit on a folded
  blanket, legs forward, bolster lengthwise over the thighs.
alternative_offer: >-
  The second way is seated on the bolster: round and lift the spine, then reach one arm overhead to
  each side. Loose fists or forearms for the wrists, more blanket for the knees.
```

## Pose: Supported Caterpillar

```yaml
id: supported-caterpillar
bilateral: false
duration_min: 4
entry: Soften the knees and let the spine round toward the bolster. Rest your chest or forehead on it. Notice the back of the body from the heels up through the spine.
target: The backs of the legs and the whole spine.
settling: Settle in at a level you could stay in for a while. If the backs of the knees pull sharply, bend them more. If the breath feels squeezed, stack the bolster taller. A 2 or a 3.
midpoint: If your mind wanders, that is normal. Come back to your breath, or to the sensation along your back.
props: Folded blanket under the hips, bolster lengthwise over the thighs, block under the bolster for height, rolled blanket available under the knees.
alternative: Knees bent with a rolled blanket underneath, or legs wide with the bolster tall for room at the belly.
exit: Roll the spine up slowly and pause with the hands behind you.
notes: Last taught in July. Back of the body untouched during the autumn arc.
```

## Transition: To Sleeping Swan

```yaml
id: transition-to-sleeping-swan
duration_min: 2
next_segment_id: sleeping-swan
setup: Sit with knees bent and feet wide, and let the knees sway side to side a few times. Then come to all fours and place the bolster lengthwise at the top of the mat and a blanket within reach.
alternative_offer: The second way is on your back, crossing one ankle over the opposite knee and drawing the legs in. Same outer hip, no kneeling.
```

## Pose: Sleeping Swan

```yaml
id: sleeping-swan
bilateral: true
duration_per_side_min: 4
side_order:
  - right
  - left
entry: From all fours, bring the named shin forward and extend the other leg behind you. Fold forward onto the bolster. Notice the outer hip and glute of the front leg.
target: The outer hip and glute of the front leg.
settling: Start shallower than you think. Scan the front knee first. If it pinches, slide a blanket under that hip or move the foot closer to you. Fold only as far as the breath stays easy. A 2 or a 3.
midpoint:
  right: Let the bolster hold your chest.
  left: Let this shape gather energy rather than spend it.
props: Bolster lengthwise under the chest, blanket under the front hip, blanket under the back knee.
alternative: Reclined figure four on the back, using the same named side.
exit: Press back to all fours and pause in neutral before switching sides.
notes: Peak. Knee sway in the transition previews the outer rotation. Dragon lesson from Oct 13 applies, so cue shallow first. Reclined figure four for late pregnancy.
```

## Transition: To Supported Sphinx

```yaml
id: transition-to-supported-sphinx
duration_min: 2
next_segment_id: supported-sphinx
setup: Slide both legs back and lie on your belly. Place the bolster crosswise under your chest so your forearms rest on the floor in front of it.
alternative_offer: The second way is lying back over the bolster lengthwise with a blanket under the head. Same gentle arch, no lying on the belly.
```

## Pose: Supported Sphinx

```yaml
id: supported-sphinx
bilateral: false
duration_min: 3
entry: Rest your chest on the bolster and your forearms on the floor, elbows near the shoulders. Let the belly and legs be heavy. Notice the low back.
target: The low back and the front of the hips.
settling: Scan the low back. If it pinches, lower the chest by sliding the bolster away or rest your forehead on your hands. A 2.
midpoint: Let the legs be heavy. Nothing needs to hold you up but the bolster.
props: Bolster crosswise under the chest, blanket under the hips if wanted.
alternative: Lying back over the bolster lengthwise with a blanket under the head.
exit: Slide the bolster away, rest the forehead on stacked hands, and sway the hips side to side.
notes: Taught week 2. Counterpose after two forward folds. Back-over-bolster alternative serves late pregnancy.
```

## Transition: To Banana

```yaml
id: transition-to-banana
duration_min: 1
next_segment_id: banana
setup: Roll onto your back with a folded blanket under the head.
alternative_offer: Lying on your side over the bolster with the top arm reaching overhead is also available.
```

## Pose: Banana

```yaml
id: banana
bilateral: true
duration_per_side_min: 3
side_order:
  - right
  - left
entry: Lying on your back, reach the arms overhead and walk the feet and upper body toward the side opposite the named side, making a gentle curve. Notice the long side of the body.
target: The side body of the named side, from the hip to the armpit.
settling: Settle in at a level you could stay in for a while. If the shoulders complain, rest the arms lower or hold the elbows. Curve only enough to feel a 2 or a 3.
midpoint:
  right: Breathe into the ribs on the long side.
  left: Let the floor hold you.
props: Folded blanket under the head.
alternative: Lying on your side over the bolster, top arm reaching overhead.
exit: Walk back to center slowly and rest flat for a breath.
notes: Taught weeks 1 and 4. Rests the hips after Swan and echoes the side reach from the opening movement.
```

## Transition: To Savasana

```yaml
id: transition-to-savasana
duration_min: 2
next_segment_id: savasana
setup: Hug the knees in and rock gently. Then bolster under the knees, blanket under the head, blanket over you.
alternative_offer: Everyone can lie on the left side with the bolster between the knees, or keep the knees bent with the feet flat.
```

## Savasana

```yaml
id: savasana
duration_min: 15
steps:
  - Propping and settling, side-lying offered to everyone
  - Five senses grounding
  - Body scan from toes to crown
  - Settling deeper with each exhale
  - Gentle awakening with gratitude for the energy gathered tonight
  - Roll to the side, press to seated, optional om, closing thank-you
wake_message: Two minutes. Carry the energy you gathered tonight into the winter.
```
