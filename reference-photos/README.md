# Studio Reference Photos

Photographs of Raw Emerald Yoga taken July 21, 2026, in the studio's actual evening light.

## Current photographs

- `mural-water-lily-raw-pine-frame-evening.jpg` — the charcoal water-lily mural in its raw pine frame: the source of `pond-charcoal`, `lotus-blush`, and the muted botanical palette.
- `plaster-wall-taper-candles-mirror-corner-evening.jpg` — troweled plaster wall, black iron candlesticks with taper candles, and the warm-edge mirror: the source of `taper-wax` and the "light pools; it does not shine" principle.
- `altar-shelf-lantern-sri-yantra-evening.jpg` — the altar shelf with wooden lantern, gold sri yantra canvas, and singing bowl: warm practical light over raw pine.
- `indigo-window-re-mark-daylight.jpg` — the indigo watercolor mark on the window: the studio's one cool note and the source of `mandala-indigo`.

These copies are privacy-scrubbed for the public repository: all EXIF metadata (including GPS coordinates) is stripped, and QR codes and instructor names on the tip sign are blurred in the altar-shelf photo. The unmodified originals remain on Clare's phone.

Keep repository files at their original resolution. Do not apply filters or color correction beyond the privacy scrub.

If more photographs are added, keep descriptive view-and-lighting filenames in the same style.

The canonical design tokens remain in `docs/design-system.md`. Photographs may refine those decisions after comparison on the Pixel 6, but should not be sampled mechanically without considering screen contrast and low-light legibility.

Note for the app's own iconography: the sri yantra and the studio's indigo mark belong to the room and to Raw Emerald Yoga. The design system's rule against mandala/lotus/om iconography inside the interface still stands; these photographs inform palette and light, not app symbols.

Do not include photographs containing identifiable students without their permission.
