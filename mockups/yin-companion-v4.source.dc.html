Theme anchor

Attention makes a path by returning. Tonight we notice the return before we try to redirect it.

Three yin principles

1 · Find the first workable edge rather than the deepest shape.

2 · Let sensation remain in the 2–5 range out of 10.

3 · Give stillness time to become informative.

Sensation scale

Aim for 2–5 of 10 — a clear, workable stretch, never a grip.

Guided / silent ratio

Guide the first four minutes, then leave longer stretches of silence.

Arrival · breathwork

Onto the back, a few unstructured breaths before naming the theme. Slow nasal breathing, longer exhale; silent after three guided rounds.

Close reference

C3Pose · expanded · overflow stress§6 · scrolls, never clipped
7:2784%
Supported Caterpillar 7:27 · 7:24–7:30 · 2:52
Entry

Extend both legs forward and soften the knees until the backs of them release toward the floor; let the spine round gradually toward the bolster, leading with the crown rather than reaching with the hands, and take three unhurried breaths before deciding how far to travel.

Target

The whole back body — heels, calves, hamstrings, and the long line of the spine.

Settling

Choose enough height under the chest and forehead that the abdomen stays soft and the breath moves freely; the sensation should sit between 2 and 5 out of 10, and if the breath shortens or the jaw grips, add height rather than insisting on depth.

Midpoint

Feel where gravity has made a path that effort no longer needs to hold.

Props & setup

Sit on the folded blanket to tip the pelvis forward; stack the bolster and blocks to meet the forehead or chest wherever they land.

Functional alternative

Bend both knees generously and support them with the rolled blanket, letting the fold happen over bent legs — an equal shape, not a lesser one.

Exit

Roll the spine upright slowly and pause with the hands behind you before the next transition.

Instructor note

Peak of the sequence; allow the longest silence here.

Close reference

Header stays compact and sticky; all eight cue fields scroll in an overflow:auto area — no overflow:hidden on authored copy (J1, §6).
